Some information about my city.

1. My favorite restaurant
My favorite restaurant is called "들깨랑 보리보리". This restaurant's signature dish is perilla kalguksu. Even on weekdays during lunchtime, people line up to eat here. There's a saying in Korea that a restaurant is considered delicious if moms line up to eat or if middle-aged men go there for a midday drink, and this place fits the bill.

2. Fun fact about city
Bucheon is known as the “City of Comics,” and it hosts Korea’s largest comic festival. There are also many institutions related to comics and animation, including museums and the Comics and Animation Promotion Agency. As you walk down the street, you’ll easily spot comic characters painted on apartment walls or character statues at the entrance to the market. One of the most interesting facts is that Dooly, the main character of the famous classic Korean comic “Baby Dinosaur Dooly,” is an honorary citizen of Bucheon and even has his own resident registration card. There’s also a famous street called Dooly Street.
